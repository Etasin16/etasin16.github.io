Fold Vergence Identifier

Two independent ways to work out where you stand on a fold: the classic field method using minor-fold asymmetry (S/Z/M), and a quantitative check using the angle between bedding and axial-planar cleavage. Use either on its own, or both together for more confidence.

Method 1

Minor-fold asymmetry (S / Z / M)

This method needs calibrating first — the direction a Z-fold "points" relative to the nearest major hinge is a local convention, not a universal rule. Set it once from a known exposure, then classify new stations against it.

Method 2

Bedding–cleavage relationship

Where axial-planar cleavage and bedding dip in roughly the same direction, their relative steepness is diagnostic: cleavage steeper than bedding indicates the anticline limb; bedding steeper than cleavage indicates the syncline limb. This also computes the bedding–cleavage intersection lineation, a common proxy for the local fold axis.
