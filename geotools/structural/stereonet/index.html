Stereonet Plotter

Enter strike/dip measurements to plot poles and great circles on an equal-area (Schmidt) or equal-angle (Wulff) lower-hemisphere stereonet. Strike follows the right-hand rule — dip direction is 90° clockwise from strike. All computation runs client-side via Pyodide; nothing is uploaded.
