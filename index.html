Stratum 01

About

I'm a 3rd-year BSc student in Geology and Mining at the University of Barishal, Bangladesh, with research interests in geomorphology, hydrogeology, and the application of geospatial tools to earth science problems.

During my undergraduate studies I contributed to two peer-reviewed publications indexed on ScienceDirect — one examining geomorphic index alterations in a coastal river system in Bangladesh, and another on hydrogeochemical characterisation of groundwater in coastal Bangladesh. My contributions involved geospatial figure preparation and graphical data visualisation, for which I was acknowledged by name in both papers. Working inside active research teams at this stage has given me a direct look at how field data becomes published scientific work.

Alongside my academic work, I built and operated a radio astronomy telescope that successfully detected the 21 cm Hydrogen Line emission of the Milky Way — an independent project spanning hardware construction, signal processing, and observational data interpretation. I've also developed several open-access geoscience tools, including a QFL Ternary Diagram Builder and Lithora, a web application for geochemical index calculations and data visualisation, built in Python.

I work with ArcGIS for spatial analysis and GIS mapping, and Python for data processing and application development — most recently learning PyVista and GemPy for 3D geological modelling and visualisation. I serve as IT Coordinator at the University of Barishal Geological Society and as a Regional Volunteer at Durbin, an astronomical society focused on public science outreach and community engagement.

Stratum 05

Outreach, Events & Community Work

Planetary Parade Observation Camp

As Durbin's regional volunteer, ran public telescope outreach sessions with the Barishal University Physics Club, teaching local students to operate Unistellar and reflecting-mirror telescopes. Sessions included live observation and imaging of the Orion Nebula, Jupiter and its four Galilean moons, Mars, and Saturn — bringing hands-on astronomy to non-specialist audiences.

Arranged by UBGS, YouthMappers BU, and EAGE-BU. Provided IT support, designed promotional materials, and coordinated event-day logistics for this multi-organisational geoscience outreach programme. YouthMappers BU is affiliated with the international YouthMappers network.
